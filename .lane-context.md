## Flow hooks — generated from the plan, ONE per flow (`@/lib/journey/flows/<Pascal>`)

Each owns its flow's forms, picks and submit plan; the page composes. The docblock is the contract:

### `lib/journey/flows/AnforderungenErfassen`
```ts
/**
 * useAnforderungenErfassenFlow — the plumbing of the flow « Anforderungen erfassen », generated from the plan.
 *
```
